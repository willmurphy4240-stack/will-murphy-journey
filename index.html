Buy Preformed Line Products (NASDAQ: PLPC)

I recommend buying PLPC because the company is the dominant, entrenched hardware supplier to an electric grid that must undergo a trillion-dollar upgrade cycle driven by AI data centers and aging infrastructure, and its specification lock-in moat, clean balance sheet, and global manufacturing expansion position it to compound that demand into long-term shareholder value.
  1. AI & data centers explode power demand
  2. The U.S. grid can't keep up
  3. Utilities must build new lines
  4. Every line needs PLP hardware
  5. Decades of runway

Why it works

  • Specification moat: utilities write PLP products into their engineering standards, so PLP can pass through costs like the 2025 steel and aluminum tariffs without losing customers.
  • Clean balance sheet: debt is under 3% of capital; dividends, buybacks, and acquisitions are funded from cash flow.
  • Manufacturing closer to customers: new plants in Poland and Spain cut shipping and tariff costs in Europe.
